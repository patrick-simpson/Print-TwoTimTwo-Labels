Chapter one

Runs the whole room

One laptop at the check-in table quietly keeps every screen in the building telling the same story.

Leaders set up once, before the first family walks in. After that the printer, the lobby TVs and the projector look after themselves, so the people at the door can look after the children.

  • Lobby slides, published once

    Type an announcement at the check-in table and every lobby screen shows it.

    Bring a friend nightPublished
    Peek under the hood
    The deck travels sealed and in ordered pieces, so every screen settles on the newest version.

  • One passphrase sets up a screen

    A replacement TV is ready after the church's passphrase is typed once. No long keys to copy by hand.

    Peek under the hood
    The passphrase derives a key (PBKDF2-SHA256) that opens a sealed setup message. The secrets land in their own storage and never leave in a settings export.

  • Keeps itself up to date

    Screens pick up a new version within minutes, and they wait until nobody is being greeted.

    Automatic
    Peek under the hood
    A small version check every three minutes. A busy screen simply asks again later, for as long as it takes.

  • First names only, sealed in transit

    Screens show a first name and nothing more, and names travel encrypted.

    Peek under the hood
    AES-256-GCM, padded so a message's length gives nothing away.

Check-in Display

The lobby says hello back.

When a label prints at the door, the lobby screen welcomes the same child a heartbeat later.

The evening's count follows the check-in desk, and a church can choose to show which children have not been checked out yet, with the safeguards below.

  • First names only
  • Birthday cheers
  • Updates itself

How the pick-up board is kept safe

  • Off until a church turns it onIt takes a deliberate choice. No setting is right for every church.
  • Stops naming anyone when only a few are leftTwo names late in the evening would point at two specific children, so it names no one.
  • First names only, alwaysNo last names, allergies or contact details ever reach a screen.
  • "Not checked out yet," never "still in the building"It shows what volunteers recorded, not a headcount, and every word says so.

Journey Display

At 6:30, the room turns to the lesson.

A leader presses one button and tonight's video begins, with captions a whole room can read.

The lesson is saved ahead of time, so a shaky evening connection doesn't stall large group. When it ends, the teaching slides follow.
